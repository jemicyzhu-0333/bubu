Use case: stylized-concept / identity-preserving expression exploration.
Asset: one polished 3 by 3 expression design sheet for the SAME existing pink mochi character. This is a concept sheet to choose facial acting vocabulary for a later 3D implementation.
Reference roles: Image 1 is the approved actual 3D model, front view. Its exact silhouette, body width/height, short rounded ear tips, two tiny attached round arms, two low rounded feet, eye positions and spacing are the identity lock. Image 2 is the original character design board: use ONLY its TOP ROW A MOCHI for the warm soft matte pink character appeal and gentle facial finish; ignore the furry B row entirely. Do not replace the existing proportions with a new character.
Layout: a square 3x3 grid, nine equal clean cells, same centered full-body character at exactly the same scale and strictly frontal camera in every cell, same neutral standing pose, same limbs, same warm light-gray background and very soft studio light/contact shadow. Generous clean margins. Keep bodies identical; vary the face only. A small dark neutral number centered below each character: 01 through 09, reading left-to-right, top-to-bottom. No other text.
Primary goal: genuinely different cute facial expressions, NOT nine sizes of the same oval eyes. Use eyelid contour, curved closed-eye lines, paired eye direction, small mouth silhouettes, and very restrained soft cheek warmth. The eyes are the character's glossy near-black eyes, not human eyes. Keep the same eye socket width and eye-center spacing; round-open eyes no larger than the reference, bilateral comparable size. No giant eyes, no squinting one eye into a tiny dot, no unequal eyeballs. Highlights follow the eye surfaces and disappear fully on closed eyes. Distinguish each expression even at tiny desktop-pet size.
01 Cute startled: open rounded eyes at reference size, alert paired highlights, a small rounded open O mouth. A brief sweet surprise, not horror.
02 Attentive focus: upper eyelids gently lower into soft focused rounded-almond eyes; a tiny short concentrated mouth. Interested rather than angry.
03 Curious / puzzled: both eyes keep matching size and glance together slightly to one side with a soft questioning lid contour, tiny gently crooked questioning mouth. No head or body turn.
04 Brief effort: paired softly pinched curved chevron eyes, a tiny compressed wavy mouth. A short adorable exertion, no teeth or aggression.
05 Being tugged / nervous anticipation: paired lifted lower eyelids and rounded upper lids produce visibly tense almond-shaped eyes with a shared sideward gaze; tiny slightly stretched open mouth. Endearing tension, never frightened or ugly.
06 Enjoying a gentle pet: completely closed soft happy eye arcs, tiny relaxed U mouth, a little diffuse cheek warmth. Serene and comfortable.
07 Quiet satisfied smile: distinct happy crescent eyes (raised lower lids), small softly open smile, subtly fuller cheeks. Keep the mouth small, no broad toothy grin.
08 Relaxed release: soft half-lidded rounded eyes still open, tiny loose resting smile, cheek warmth returns almost to neutral. Calm, alert enough, visibly different from full closed pleasure.
09 Sleepy: paired heavy drooping upper eyelids leaving narrow soft dark eye slits, tiny sleepy yawn mouth. Drowsy rather than sad.
Preserve: single broad integrated mochi body/head, no neck, same short rounded ear tips, arms and feet. Powder-matte warm pink body, glossy eyes only. No body squash, no pose changes, no long limbs, no ears changing shape. No accessories, props, hands petting it, motion lines, symbols, sweat drops, tears, eyelashes, eyebrows, nose, muzzle, teeth, tongue, white human eyeballs, fur, decorative hearts, or duplicated characters within a cell. Do not add separate red cheek stickers. This must look like nine carefully designed facial states of one consistent 3D character, not nine different mascots.
