# Sip-tea scene contact draft (non-executable)

Status: body-art direction pending. This is not a new owner, runtime schema, clock or admitted scene.

Inputs: one explicit sip-tea request, source food identity (coffee/milk if supplied by the existing food ritual), current valid actor capability, cup resource revision. Food consumption transaction remains outside this research scene; an interrupted visual must not retry or duplicate consumption.

Resources: one action-local cup, three meshes, two materials, four named contact frames. No wearing/accessory side effects. User's existing background stays inherited.

Stages without art timing defaults:
- Enter: acquire cup successfully before visible commitment; cup begins on a declared support surface. Character uses a reviewed preparation pose. No prop teleport from origin, no repeated acquisition during hold.
- Grip: admitted hand surface anchor meets cup handle/support frames. Only then can the cup leave support; otherwise the scene is rejected and cleans up.
- Lift: cup follows reviewed continuous path, local grip frames remain in contact. Full swept clearance required, including shoulder patch and face.
- Sip: cup near-rim frame meets an approved mouth contact anchor. Explicitly permit only local lip contact; ceramic body cannot intersect face/eyes. Tea surface stays inside cup. Any drinking tilt needs gravity-consistent liquid orientation or conservative bounded tilt.
- Return: cup returns to declared support before hands release. Final body is the original rest or an admitted interaction takeover pose.
- Exit: remove action-local cup only after its visible exit/support policy; destroy local resources once.

Face policy: all18 approved source paths are available, but no new ID mapping or artistic durations are silently chosen. V1 surprised→hungry→satisfied labels are semantic source cues, not face IDs. All events must enter existing P1 complete batches; no direct morph writes or independent timeline.

Interrupt: drag/pet ownership supersedes only through existing host arbitration, with no simultaneous body writer. A visible interrupted return may be possible only if the reviewed contact path can complete safely; otherwise a separately approved stage cleanup is required. Hidden/context-loss/dispose removes cup and invalidates old request lifetime; late completion cannot reattach it. The scene must not resume old coffee activity after a replacement request.

Gates still open: approved extended-arm appearance; exact rig/asset profile; runtime host body ownership handoff; full path/continuous collision; reduced-motion pose; failure and repeated-entry resource counts; browser and platform visual verification. Current contact-frame utilities are pure geometry helpers and do not satisfy these gates by themselves.
